## About section (first person)
I'm a New Zealand pharmacist and the founder of NearMissPro.

After years behind the dispensary bench, one thing always frustrated me: we're brilliant at catching near misses, but the logbook never actually helped us prevent the next one. The same slips kept coming back — same look-alike box, same busy Friday — and nothing turned all that effort into change.

So I'm building NearMissPro: a simple tool for NZ community pharmacies to log a near miss in seconds, surface the recurring patterns, and see plainly whether the changes they make actually reduce them. It suggests practical fixes grounded in NZ best practice — but every recommendation is advisory, and the pharmacist stays in complete control. No blame, no names. Just prevention.

It's in early development, built with real NZ pharmacies in mind. If you work in community pharmacy and care about patient safety, I'd love to connect.

## Experience entry — "Founder · NearMissPro"
