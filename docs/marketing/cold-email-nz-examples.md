**Subject:** The near miss that keeps coming back

Kia ora pharmacy team,

I'm Kim, a NZ pharmacist. I'm reaching out to a few community pharmacies because I've built something I think could genuinely help — and I'd really value your honest take.

You'll know these ones. **Allergron** (nortriptyline) reached for when the script said **Allersoothe** — two names a syllable apart, a shelf apart. Or a blister sheet of **fluoxetine** picked instead of **tramadol**, two strips that look almost the same. Caught at the check, sorted, everyone moves on.

We catch these every day. The trouble is, the logbook rarely helps us stop the *next* one — so when that same look-alike turns up again a few weeks later, no one's lined them up to notice it's the third time.

That's the gap I've been trying to close. NearMissPro keeps it simple:

- **Log a near miss in about ten seconds** — a few taps at the bench. No forms, nothing to write up later.
- **See the ones that keep repeating** — a look-alike that's happened three times gets flagged, not forgotten.
- **Get the monthly review done for you** — the write-up's already there, so the team meeting just agrees the fix and closes the loop.

No blame, no names, no patient details collected — and if someone accidentally types one into the notes, the app catches it.

It's still in development. Everyone gets the first 3 months free, and I'm after a few NZ pharmacies to have an early look and tell me honestly what works and what doesn't.

Keen for a look when it's ready? Join the waitlist here: https://tally.so/r/D4YgQl — or just reply to this email and I'll be in touch.

And if it's not for you, no worries at all — reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.
