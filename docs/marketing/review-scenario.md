# Scenario — the monthly review & staff meeting

Simple, clear, first-person. Shows the fast review and how the month comes out
digestible enough to drive one clear decision. Numbers illustrative.
Usable as a LinkedIn post, a Facebook follow-up, or a one-pager for a pharmacy.

---

The last Friday of the month — team meeting after close.

Before, I'd spend the night before going through the near-miss book by hand, trying to add it all up. By the meeting all I could really say was "we had a few, watch the look-alikes." Nothing changed.

Now I open NearMissPro and press one button. Two minutes later it gives me the whole month in plain words:

"18 near misses, down from 24. Most were at labelling. Atorvastatin came up 4 times — same look-alike box as last month. The shelf change we made in May worked: metoprolol dropped from 5 to 1."

I read that out. Five minutes, and the whole team gets it:
- Still happening: atorvastatin, 4 times
- Working: metoprolol, our fix helped
- To do: split the atorvastatin strengths — [name] to sort it, check next month

One problem, one win, one thing to fix. That's the meeting.

The write-up went from an evening to two minutes — and the meeting went from "be careful" to a real plan.
