"No harm done."

Three words we say in the dispensary more than we'd like to admit.

Every day, pharmacy teams catch near misses — the wrong strength, the look-alike box, the typo spotted at the final check. We fix it, feel the relief, and move on.

But a near miss isn't luck. It's a warning. The same look-alike packaging, the same understaffed afternoon, the same interruption at the bench — quietly telling you where the next real error is going to come from.

The safest pharmacies I know aren't the ones with fewer near misses. They're the ones that notice the pattern and change something — before one reaches a patient.

That's the hard part. Not catching the near miss — we're good at that. It's turning the logbook into a change that actually stops it happening again.

So I've started building something for it: NearMissPro. A simple way for NZ pharmacies to log a near miss in seconds, spot the patterns, and see whether last month's change actually reduced them. No blame, no names — just prevention.

It's not live yet. I'm building it with real NZ pharmacies in mind, and I'd genuinely value your input. Waitlist pharmacies get free early access while I build it (link in the comments).

But first, I'd really like to know: how does your pharmacy handle near misses right now? Does anything turn that logbook into real change?
