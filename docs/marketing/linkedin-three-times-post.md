Here's how a near miss hides in plain sight.

Someone picks amlodipine when the script says amiodarone. Caught at the final check. Fixed, relief, move on.

A couple of weeks later, someone else does the same thing. Caught again. Sorted again.

And a week after that — a third time. Each one caught. Each one dealt with in the moment.

But because we catch each one and move on, nobody clocks that it's the same near miss, three times over. The third looks just like the first.

That's how a pattern hides — not because anyone's careless, but because we handle each one on its own and never line them up.

If the same near miss happened three times in your dispensary last month, would you know?
