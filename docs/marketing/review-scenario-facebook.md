## THE POST

Kia ora everyone,

A quick one about the monthly near-miss review.

It used to be I'd spend the night before going through the near-miss book by hand, adding it all up. By the meeting all I could really say was "we had a few, watch the look-alikes." Nothing really changed.

These days I press one button and two minutes later I've got the whole month in plain words:

"18 near misses, down from 24. Most at labelling. Atorvastatin came up 4 times — same look-alike box as last month. The shelf change we made in May worked: metoprolol dropped from 5 to 1."

Five minutes at the meeting and the whole team's on the same page:
- Still happening: atorvastatin
- Working: metoprolol, our fix helped
- To do: split the atorvastatin strengths, someone to sort it, check next month

One problem, one win, one thing to fix. Meeting done.

(That's the tool I've been building — NearMissPro. The numbers are just an example.)

How long does your monthly review take at the moment — and does it actually lead to a change, or just a "be careful everyone"?
